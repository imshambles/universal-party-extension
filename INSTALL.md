# Universal Party — Install & Use

Watch anything together, in sync. Works on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Hotstar, and YouTube.

This is an **unpacked Chrome extension** (not on the Web Store yet), so it loads in Developer Mode. Takes about a minute.

## Install (one time)

1. **Unzip** the file you received. You'll get a folder named `universal-party-extension`.
2. Open your browser's extensions page:
   - Chrome → `chrome://extensions`
   - Brave → `brave://extensions`
   - Edge → `edge://extensions`
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle, top-right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped `universal-party-extension` folder.
5. (Recommended) Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and **pin** Universal Party so its icon is always visible.

## Start a watch party

1. Open a video on a supported site (e.g. a YouTube video).
2. Click the **Universal Party** icon.
3. Type your name → **Start watch party**. The invite link is copied to your clipboard automatically.
4. Send that link to whoever you want to watch with.

## Join someone's party

Either:
- **Open the invite link** they sent you — it drops you into the same video at their timestamp, or
- Click the **Universal Party** icon → paste the link in **"Paste invite link…"** → **Join**.

Once you're in, the sidebar appears on the right: play/pause/seek stay in sync for everyone, plus group video chat and text chat.

## Good to know

- **Allow camera & mic** when the browser asks — that powers the group video chat. (You can mute either from the sidebar.)
- The green dot in the sidebar header means you're connected.
- The backend runs on a free tier that "sleeps" when idle, so the **first** join after a quiet period can take ~50 seconds to connect. After that it's instant.
- Everyone needs the extension installed to join a party.
